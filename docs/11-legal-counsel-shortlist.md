## Engagement options to request

For each firm, ask for:

1. **Fixed-fee review** of a named scope. For example, a regulatory memo on air ticketing, or a data-protection gap review.
2. **Phased review.** Phase 1 covers a short scoping memo and is paid on delivery. Each later phase needs approval before it starts.
3. **Hourly or retainer** rates, with an estimated total for the first phase and a monthly cap.

Each quote should state: named lawyers, conflicts check result, expected deliverables, timelines, and what is excluded.

## Scope to put to counsel (from docs/10)

1. Data protection: NDPA 2023 obligations, travel-document handling, cross-border transfers.
2. Consumer protection and terms of service for a booking platform.
3. Air ticketing and the agency or licensing position with the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority.
4. Payments: NGN authorisation and capture, licensing or partner status, refund rules.
5. Trademark: "GO" name and the logo, including the risk around the G letterform.
6. Corporate matters: company registration and structure.

Statute references in docs/10 are unconfirmed and should be checked by counsel.
