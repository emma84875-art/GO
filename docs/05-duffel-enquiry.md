## Full enquiry (for the email follow-up, or the form if there is room)

**Subject:** Enquiry: Nigerian merchant eligibility, ticketing and fees for a premium flight booking platform

Dear Duffel Sales Team,

We are [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], a company incorporated in Nigeria (CAC registration [RC NUMBER]). We are building a premium flight booking platform that will launch in Nigeria, with international departures from launch. We are evaluating Duffel as our first flight supplier and would like written answers to the questions below before we start technical onboarding.

**1. Merchant eligibility**
- Can a Nigerian-incorporated company contract with Duffel as a seller? If so, which entity documents and beneficial-ownership information do you require?
- Are there any restrictions on Nigerian-based sellers, or on selling flights to customers resident in Nigeria?
- What are the onboarding steps, typical timeline, and who in your team manages the process?

**2. Onboarding and sandbox**
- Can we access a sandbox with search, pricing, order creation and ticket issuance?
- What must be completed before sandbox access, and before production access?
- Are there any integration reviews, certification tests or go-live checks?

**3. Ticket issuance**
- Under Managed Content, does Duffel issue tickets on our behalf for itineraries that start in Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt and others), including international departures?
- Which airlines are covered for ticketing under Managed Content, and which require our own accreditation or a direct agreement with the airline?
- How are ticket numbers returned, and what happens if ticketing fails after payment is taken?
- What are the rules, fees and timelines for changes, refunds, voids and cancellations, and who is responsible for airline refunds?
- Does Duffel require a particular fare-rule or disclosure format that we must show customers?

**4. Commercial fees**
- We understand the pay-as-you-go pricing is $3.00 per confirmed order, 1% of order value for Managed Content, $2.00 per paid ancillary, a search overage above a 1500:1 search-to-book ratio, and a 2% FX fee where conversion is needed. Please confirm these figures apply to Nigerian sellers.
- Are there minimum monthly commitments, platform fees, or setup fees?
- Are there ticketing, change, or void fees that are not on the pricing page?
- What does the Enterprise tier cost, and what does it add? Under what conditions can we use our own IATA accreditation?

**5. Payment and settlement**
- In which currencies does Duffel invoice us and settle with us? Can we be invoiced and paid in US dollars?
- Can settlement go to a Nigerian bank account? If so, which banks and which FX arrangements apply?
- How are customer refunds, chargebacks and disputes handled? Are we the merchant of record for the customer?
- How often are fees invoiced, and what detail do invoices include?

**6. International flight coverage**
- Please provide the list of airlines available for search and booking from Nigerian airports, including carriers serving London, Dubai, New York and other long-haul destinations.
- Which of these airlines are available via direct API connection, and which via aggregated content?
- How current is the inventory, and how do you handle fare changes between search and payment?

**7. Data protection and legal**
- What data do you hold about our customers, where is it stored, and how long is it retained?
- Will you act as a processor or controller for passenger data? Please share your data processing agreement.
- Can you share your services agreement and any Nigeria-specific terms?

We would appreciate written answers and any documents that help us assess eligibility. We have not yet committed to any agreement with Duffel or any other supplier. Our next step depends on your answers.
